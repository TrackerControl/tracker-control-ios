Method

From download to attribution.

  1. 01Download

    Collect a version of the app from the UK App Store.

  2. 02Run

    Use the app in a controlled iOS test session.

  3. 03Identify

    Inspect embedded software signatures and permissions.

  4. 04Attribute

    Connect recognised trackers with their companies and jurisdictions.

Research provenance

Open work, clear limits.

The analysis technique shown here was developed in Konrad Kollnig's PhD research at Oxford. Read related research at Platform Control and inspect the iOS implementation in the TrackerControl iOS repository.

Detection identifies embedded software. It does not establish that tracking occurred.
